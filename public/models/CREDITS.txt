Ward art & asset credits
========================

Original Ward geometry (heroes, procedural monsters, FX)
--------------------------------------------------------
Hand-authored Three.js meshes for all playable races and wave-1
procedural foes (wolf/werewolf silhouette, demon/imp, slime, gargoyle,
spider, cultist, sprig, whelp, hillock, lurker, lumen, flicker).
Fantasy archetypes only — no third-party game IP.

Environment PBR (Poly Haven, CC0)
---------------------------------
https://polyhaven.com
- castle_brick_broken_06 — dungeon ground (4K maps + 8K diffuse)
- cracked_red_ground — hell ground (4K maps + 8K diffuse)
- cobblestone_floor_03 — road (4K maps)

KayKit (CC0) — dungeon dressing & undead GLBs
---------------------------------------------
Kay Lousberg — https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets
Characters / undead already in public/models (hound, sentinel, archer,
brute, knight, rogue, mage) and optional race GLB references under
public/models/races/. Runtime heroes prefer original Ward meshes.
Dungeon Remastered props under public/models/props/ (chests, barrels,
banners, rubble, torches, tables, arches, doorways, stairs).

No files from Redeev, Oniro, or other commercial games are included.
